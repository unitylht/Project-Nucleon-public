Where the demo starts

The site starts clean: no rules are saved and nothing has been decided. Until you save rules on the Control panel, the agent decides nothing and every request comes to you.
  1. Sign in at nucleon.dracooz.top as the Owner. Open Control panel and save your rules. The quickest way is Quick setup; the "starting rules" that the results above assume are listed in the manual, section 6.
  2. Your staff (or the person helping you) opens the Assistant as Staff and types a request, one new chat for each customer. For example:
    Bayview Gelato Co asks to move the due date of invoice DEMO-301 from 18 October 2026 to 10 November 2026.
  3. Look at the card under the assistant's answer. The card is the result; the assistant's own words may vary.
  4. As the Owner, open Decide. Requests waiting for you are at the top; decisions the agent made are below, for you to agree with or reverse.
  5. Change one rule on the Control panel and send a similar request again to see the result change.

The exact sentence to type for every customer, and what should happen, are in the Nucleon Operating Manual, section 6, "Scenarios to try".

What this data cannot show you

  • It is not your business. Six customers and amounts of a few hundred dollars show how Nucleon behaves; they do not show whether its decisions match yours on your own customers.
  • Each customer's request can be decided once. Asking again returns the same stored result. To start over, the data has to be reset (ask Hanting; it takes a minute).
  • The date is fixed at 24 October 2026, so nothing becomes more overdue while you test.
