## Supersession clarification
Old reading: 'Every credit request must always be decided by a human Owner.' **Superseded as a product requirement by PM-001.**
Still valid: if policy has not explicitly delegated authority for a given business action, backend must not autonomously approve it. Current `OWNER_REVIEW_REQUIRED` guard remains until replaced by a tested, Owner-authorised policy mechanism; never simply remove the safeguard.

## How to make a proposal binding
Record exact scope, decision-maker and date; list policy bounds, evidence, expected failure cases and exceptions; change proposal to confirmed only on explicit user/Owner/team authorisation appropriate to that decision. Never infer consensus from an AI-written draft.
