# Product design meeting follow-up — 26 October 2026
Provenance: user-supplied 66-minute meeting transcript (two principal speakers, not confidently identified here) + **subsequent explicit user instructions in chat**. Transcription can be imperfect. This is a decision/evidence digest, not a claim of formal unanimous group vote.

## Meeting insights
- Team discussion strongly favours shared learning across customers over per-customer cold start; otherwise Owner must repeatedly handle requests for every new customer.
- Similar reviewed cases and low exposure were discussed as a route to automated internal decisions. Cross-customer analogy requires relevant current-customer evidence, not blind generalisation.
- Owner can be proactively offered a review of increasing a policy cap after enough **verified comparable decisions** and real outcome evidence; never auto-enlarge authority.
- New-customer evidence can be gathered with questions and suitable documents, including by asking Staff. Actual sources and privacy constraints remain open.
- Staff reluctance to take personal responsibility for credit decisions is a central original bottleneck. Replacing Owner approval with Staff approval per case may merely move a queue.
- Distinguish automatic internal decisions from manually sending notifications and other external commitments.
- Owner rationale, decision outcomes and similarity should be remembered, while any claims about ML confidence from payment-based proxy labels require strong caveats.
- Messaging should distinguish a narrow credit POC from possible future decision-domain expansion.
- Team presenters need an accurate deployed-versus-roadmap brief, not an unstable many-page technical manual.

## User-confirmed follow-up decisions (later than meeting)
1. AI may make **binding internal credit decisions** when a versioned Owner delegation policy authorises that class of action; Staff does not need to re-approve each case. A human may supervise and carry out downstream tasks separately.
2. Long-term, Agent may replace more Staff work in this category; **not a current POC acceptance requirement**.
3. AI must be able to seek additional evidence from Staff, not only Owner or customer.
4. A low-exposure new customer with verified deposit and sufficient substitute evidence is a representative target for autonomous handling, provided explicit Owner trial policy.
5. The existing console UI is to be discarded; preserve 'Key Facts' due/today/requested timeline concept. New console has not begun; future 'Decision Handling' dashboard illustration is liked.
6. Save feature catalogue and two-horizon message for speeches and marketing.
7. User intends a substantial Claude Code refactor before rehearsal. **Intent is not proof of completion**. Three presentation handoff briefs should be produced *after* the refactor, not before.
8. Hoang's Python ML training/cleaning scripts are distinct from Nucleon's deterministic accounting/date calculations. `NTK Project Summary.docx` is outdated and must not govern current requirements.
