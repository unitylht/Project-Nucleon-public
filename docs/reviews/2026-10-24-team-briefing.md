# Nucleon Week 7 briefing (one page) and slide outline

Written for the NTK project team ahead of the Week 7 proof-of-concept presentation. Fictional and anonymised data only. Dates are Sydney time.

## One-page briefing

**The problem.** NTK's owner approves every request from a customer to pay an invoice later. Each one means finding the invoice and the customer's history in the ledger, judging the risk, deciding, and telling staff what to reply. The team's evidence: the owner interviews and the anonymised twelve-month ledger (21 customers, 137 invoices, 113 payments), where late payment is common (for the two largest customers, 12 of 16 and 17 of 19 receipts arrived while something was already overdue) and the records alone do not say what is still owed on most invoices.

**The solution, at a high level.** Nucleon keeps the ledger as records (imported from the cleaned workbook, or entered by hand) and adds an agent that staff and the owner talk to. Staff paste the customer's words; the agent finds the customer and invoice, opens a case, and asks code to work out the position: what is owed, what is overdue, the payment history, and each of the owner's limits as pass, fail or unknown. Inside the limits, staff act themselves; outside them, the case goes to the owner, who can ask staff for more, give guidance, and approve or decline with a reason. Everything is recorded with who did it, and the calculation behind every assessment can be expanded and is frozen with each decision.

**Evidence that it works.** The Lab is deployed and was tested in the browser with the local model on isolated copies of the ledger: import with reconciliation (0 discrepancies against the workbook on the corrected validator), a routine request acted on within authority, an exception with concern, guidance, follow-up and the owner's decision, reason retrieval, owner-private context kept from staff, same-day and above-limit boundaries, changed evidence re-checked at execution, authority changes applied only to new assessments. The test counts at the deployed build are in `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`.

**What is live, what is fixture-only, what is future.** Live on the Lab: import, retrieval, assessment with calculation details, acting within authority, escalation and the concern loop, decisions with frozen basis, the AI Agent testing login. Demonstrated only with fictional fixtures: same-day invoices, invoices with no due date, above-limit exposure, stale-tab refusal. Future work: sending replies (today they are drafts), payment-terms arithmetic from the ledger (terms are read from the cleaned file), an approval predictor, hosted models, real-business records.

**Accurate statements to make.** Hoang's ledger replay and feature work (what is overdue and for how long, receipts made while something was overdue, relationship length) informed the implemented calculation layer and its feature names. The trained approval classifiers are not running. Recommendations come from deterministic calculations and the owner-configured policy checks; no validated approval-confidence percentage is available. Calculation rules are reproducible within a version (calc-v1), and humans control policy changes through the owner's Authority panel. Replies are drafts, not sent messages. Technical tests do not establish measured business time savings.

**Input requirements and known limitations.** The raw accounting export is not importable as exported: it has no credit-term or due-date column and groups rows under customer headers. The supported input is the cleaned workbook: one row per transaction, a customer on every row, invoice number, document date, debit and credit, a credit term or due date, and a transaction type. Netting and adjustment rows are left out, so customers 0008 and 0017 do not reconcile to the ledger by VND 59,700,000 and VND 18,425,880; a payment that names an invoice issued later stays unmatched and is applied oldest first; nine payment references name invoices that sit in the opening balances. Where the records do not say what is still owed or when an invoice is due, the product says "not recorded" or "unknown" and sends the case to the owner.

## Slide-content outline (title plus four content slides)

**Title slide.** Nucleon: payment-extension requests handled with the owner's limits, not instead of the owner. Team, course, date.

**Slide 1: Problem and evidence.** The owner decides every extension by hand; the ledger shows frequent late payment (two largest customers: 12 of 16 and 17 of 19 receipts late) and rarely records what is still owed. Evidence: owner interviews, the anonymised twelve-month ledger, the validator report.

**Slide 2: Solution and evidence it works.** Records plus an agent; code computes, people decide. Screenshots: the assessment card with the owner's limits, the expanded Calculation details, a saved decision with its frozen basis. Evidence: browser-tested journeys on isolated data, import reconciled with 0 discrepancies, test counts at the deployed build.

**Slide 3: Agentic business process and human control points.** Trigger: a customer's words pasted by staff. The agent perceives (finds customer, invoice, history), decides (calculation plus policy checks, no predictor), acts through tools (opens the case, prepares decisions, drafts replies) and stops at explicit approval points: staff press Act within authority; the owner presses Confirm; only the owner changes the limits; replies stay drafts.

**Slide 4: Build environment and component responsibilities.** Records service (Python standard library, SQLite): records, the fixed replay and policy checks, decisions, audit trail. Lab (Starlette, Pydantic AI, React): the conversation, guards on the model's words, cards. Local model (Gemma 4 26B via a home gateway): reads words and chooses tools; never computes money or creates authority. Importer and validator: the cleaned workbook in, an independent check against it. Deployment: one home server, two Lab halves, backups before every deploy.
