# Consent: recorded, screen-shared test session

**Project:** Nucleon (university course project, INFS3605). **Session:** a practice test of a prototype credit-decision assistant, about 45-60 minutes.

## What you are agreeing to

1. **What we record.** Your shared screen and your voice during the session, plus the notes the facilitator takes (what you did, how long it took, what you said). We do not record your camera unless you tell us it is fine.
2. **Fictional data only.** Every customer, invoice and amount in the test is made up. Please do not type or show real customer, bank or business information. If you do by mistake, tell us and we will remove it.
3. **How it is stored.** The recording and notes are kept on the project team's own computer, are not uploaded to any shared drive or cloud service, and are seen only by team members working on this project. We keep them until the course assessment is finished, then delete them.
4. **De-identification.** In anything we share (reports, slides, the team's feedback table) you appear only as "Owner" or "Staff". We remove your name, your business name and anything else that identifies you. A short quote may be used without your name.
5. **Your right to stop.** You can pause, skip a step or stop at any time, without giving a reason and without any consequence. You can ask us afterwards to delete your recording and notes, and we will.
6. **No authorisation implied.** This is a simulation. Nothing you do in the test approves real credit, is sent to a real customer, or commits your business to anything. Taking part does not mean you or your business endorse, will buy or will use the product.
7. **You are testing the product, not being tested.** If something is confusing or the assistant gets it wrong, that is useful for us.

If you have questions or want your data deleted, contact the facilitator who invited you.
